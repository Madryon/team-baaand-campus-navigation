Problem Framing

The Real Challenge & Revised How Might We

The Core Disconnect

Commercial map applications (e.g. Google Maps) terminate at the broad campus perimeter or drop pins in empty quads. They fail completely at the "last 100 meters": missing gate numbers (Gates 1–6), multi-department complexes (e.g. OR Dept located inside Faculty of Arts), and specific floor/room allocations.

Revised HMW (Team Consensus)

"How might we help new students find the exact department, building or gate with clear, reliable information, so they do not depend on a guard or another student being physically around?"
Target Users

User Roles & Behavioral Segments

New Student (Primary)

New Master's Student

First 2 weeks on campus. Experiences disorientation finding the exact department. Needs high-touch landmark cues, verified gate numbers, and walking times.

Visitor (Secondary)

First-Time Visitor / Parent

Attending an academic seminar or administrative office. Unfamiliar with university jargon. Needs public parking locations, Central Reception desk, and accessible entrances.

Campus Insider (Anti-Persona)

Final Year Student / Scholar

Knows every shortcut across campus. Finds turn-by-turn landmark banners annoying visual noise. Demands an Insider Mode to suppress guidance prompts.

Component & Spatial Hierarchy

  • 4pt/8pt Spatial Rhythm: --space-1 (4px) to --space-8 (32px).
  • Touch Target Standard: Guaranteed minimum 44x44px across all 56 interactive buttons.
  • Zero Hardcoded Styling: 100% tokenized via tokens.css and tokens.json.

Evaluation

Expert Heuristic & Accessibility Audit (v1)

Evaluated against Nielsen’s 10 Usability Heuristics and WCAG 2.2 AA standards:

ID Heuristic Sev Problem Identified in v1
H1 Visibility of Status 2 Step counter lacked linear graphical progress bar of route.
H2 Match Real World 2 Generic dark box used for entrance instead of real archway sketch.
H3 Control & Freedom 3 Trapped navigation: lacked 1-tap "Exit Route" action in HUD.
H4 Consistency 2 Unstyled native HTML dropdown for origin selection.
H5 Error Prevention 2 Search typos caused zero-result dead ends without suggestions.
H6 Recognition vs Recall 3 Home cards lacked direct gate tags and estimated walking minutes.
H9 Error Recovery 3 Off-route warning was a temporary 3s toast easily missed outdoors.
Remediation

Iterating to Prototype v2: 10/10 Fixes Landed

Navigation & Orientation Fixes

  • Linear Progress Bar (H1): 4-segment active bar shows remaining distance.
  • Illustrated Archway (H2): Red-brick archway drawing + "✓ Verified Physical Signboard".
  • Exit Route HUD Button (H3): Dedicated [ ✕ Exit Route ] button.
  • Interactive Origin Selector (H4): 1-tap pill buttons for Gates 4, 1, 2, and GPS.

Search, Safety & Cognitive Calm

  • Fuzzy Search Suggestions (H5): Auto-suggests OR Dept on typo queries.
  • Gate Badges on Home (H6): Terracotta gate tags and ⏱ 3 min walk indicators.
  • Role Persistence (H7): LocalStorage writes preserve mode across sessions.
  • Persistent Off-Route Banner (H9): Sticky warning with 1-tap [ Re-center ] action.
  • Nearest Guard Cabin (H10): Real-time "Nearest: Gate 4 Cabin (120m)" badge.

Strategic Roadmap

Strategic Roadmap (2027 Vision)

Pillar 1: Physical Signage QR Deep-Linking

Install durable metal placards at Gates 1–6 with direct QR codes that immediately launch the web app with the user's starting gate pre-calibrated.

Pillar 2: Indoor BLE Wayfinding

Deploy battery-efficient Bluetooth beacons across Faculty of Arts corridors to provide 2.5D floor switching and indoor seminar hall routing.

Pillar 3: Real-Time Transit Telemetry

Partner with campus e-rickshaws and Smart Bike docks to stream live vehicle locations and dock availability.

Pillar 4: Participatory PwD Studies

Conduct on-campus route auditing with permanent wheelchair users and mobility-impaired students.
